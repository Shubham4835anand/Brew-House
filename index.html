ABOUT US

ENGINEERED EXTRACTION.
ZERO COMPROMISE.

Mocharé was built to dismantle industrial shortcuts. We roast farm-direct micro-lots in 10kg cast-iron drums daily, profiling thermal rise down to 0.1°C per second for profound clarity and delicate natural aromatics.

From volcanic partner estates in Huila, Sidama, and Chiriquí to our quiet walnut workspaces, every extraction is dialed to honor the producer's craft and spark your deep flow state.
